kuber

A private, single-user personal finance tool.

kuber is a personal project run by its author for their own household finances. It gathers the author's own account balances and statements, and projects savings over time. It is not a public service: there are no other users and no sign-ups.

To read the author's emailed bank statements, kuber uses Google's read-only Gmail access (gmail.readonly). See the privacy policy for how that data is handled.
